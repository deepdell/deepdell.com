03 / QUESTIONS

Questions, answered.

Can I cancel at any time?

Yes. Plans run month to month with no contract.

What counts as a support hour?

Fixes, consultation calls, configuration, monitoring and building automations all count.

What if I need more hours?

Extra hours can be added at a discounted rate, or you can upgrade your plan at any time.

Which platforms do you support?

Shopify, Amazon, eBay, Walmart, HubSpot, Google Ads, Meta, Snapchat, Google Analytics and custom APIs. Ask if yours is not listed.

Do you offer local currency billing?

Yes. Prices are shown in your local currency, adjusted to local purchasing power, and billed in that currency. Use the selector above to change it.

Is there a free call?

Yes. The first 20-minute discovery call is free.
