Privacy & analytics.

How DEEPDELL uses website analytics and your consent choices.

Google Analytics

DEEPDELL uses Google Analytics 4 to understand aggregate website usage, such as pages viewed and general interaction patterns, so we can improve the site.

Ahrefs Analytics

DEEPDELL also uses Ahrefs Analytics for aggregate website analytics and experience improvement. It is loaded only after you allow analytics.

Consent

Analytics is off by default for new visitors. The Google Analytics and Ahrefs Analytics scripts are not loaded, and no request is made to Google or Ahrefs for analytics, until you select Allow analytics. If you reject analytics, they are never loaded.

Change your choice

You can clear this site's browser storage to see the consent choice again, or contact DEEPDELL if you need assistance with a privacy request.
