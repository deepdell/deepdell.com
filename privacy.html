Privacy & analytics.

How DEEPDELL uses website analytics and your consent choices.

Google Analytics

DEEPDELL uses Google Analytics 4 to understand aggregate website usage, such as pages viewed and general interaction patterns, so we can improve the site.

Consent

Analytics storage is denied by default for new visitors. The Google tag is present so consent can be respected and measured correctly, but analytics storage remains denied until you select Allow analytics. If you reject analytics, analytics storage remains denied.

Change your choice

You can clear this site's browser storage to see the consent choice again, or contact DEEPDELL if you need assistance with a privacy request.
